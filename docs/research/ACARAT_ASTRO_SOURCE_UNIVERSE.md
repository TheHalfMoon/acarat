# Acarat Astro Source Universe

**Status:** PUBLIC PLANNING INPUT  
**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Purpose:** seed Astro with a broad but governed research universe before it reshapes the canonical Acarat plan.

## 1. Non-negotiable source rule

A source list is not an architecture.

Astro must:

1. inspect live source truth;
2. identify the exact capability sought;
3. compare at least one native/simple alternative;
4. pin exact revision/release/path before adoption;
5. verify license, NOTICE, assets, models, datasets, and transitive dependencies separately;
6. record security/privacy/data-rights implications;
7. classify the source as AUTHORITY / REFERENCE / DEPENDENCY_CANDIDATE / ADAPT / COPY_SELECTIVE / REJECT / RIGHTS_REQUIRED;
8. keep Acarat-owned domain contracts primary;
9. reject overlap and unnecessary platform absorption;
10. preserve exit/rollback strategy.

Founder permission makes a source eligible for review; it does not erase third-party obligations.

## 3. Saudi government and authoritative sources

Astro must reverify all official sources at planning time and again before implementation.

### Core real-estate authorities

- Real Estate General Authority (REGA): platform licensing, FAL/brokerage rules, advertising/platform requirements, technology integration, laws/regulations, complaints and regulatory guidance.
- REGA Real Estate Indicators: sales, rental, city/neighborhood comparison, interactive maps, market methodology and outlier handling.
- Ejar: direct digital integration with real-estate marketing platforms, rental contract lifecycle, renewal and parties/contract state.
- Real Estate Registry (RER): first registration, ownership transfer, split/merge, rights/restrictions/responsibilities, spatial registration model.
- National Address / SPL Developer Portal: address search, verification, geocoding/reverse geocoding, regions/cities/districts, POIs/nearest POIs, map APIs.
- Balady / municipal urban maps: roads, land use, plot context, building regulations, public/service POIs, municipal planning context.
- REGA Geospatial Real Estate Portal: plots, POIs, off-plan projects, brokerage offices, registration areas.

### Additional Saudi sources Astro must research

- Nafath / National Single Sign-On integration requirements.
- Saudi Business Center platform authentication requirements.
- Saudi Authority for Data and Artificial Intelligence / PDPL / NDMO data-governance requirements.
- National Cybersecurity Authority controls applicable to the platform.
- Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) rules relevant to future mortgage/financing/payment integrations.
- Real Estate Development Fund and Sakani for home-finance/support journeys.
- ZATCA Real Estate Transaction Tax rules/calculators/data interfaces relevant to buyer cost scenarios.
- General Authority for Statistics real-estate price indexes and demographic/economic datasets.
- Saudi Open Data Portal for lawful reusable datasets.
- General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information / national geospatial standards and reference systems.
- Ministry of Justice / Najiz property/deed flows where relevant.
- Off-Plan Sale and Lease / Wafi authority and project/developer licensing.
- Ministry of Municipalities and Housing datasets/services relevant to planning, permits, building rules and urban context.

These are authorities/data sources, not code donors.

## 21. Astro-discovered source expansion

Astro is explicitly authorized and required to expand this source universe.

Discovery rules:

1. Search GitHub, official documentation, standards, papers and current competitor product docs.
2. Prefer primary/official sources over summaries.
3. Prefer mature, active, well-governed open-source projects.
4. Record why every newly added source materially closes a gap.
5. Reject duplicates and fashionable but unnecessary infrastructure.
6. For algorithms/ML, search recent peer-reviewed or strong technical evidence as well as code.
7. For Saudi legal/regulatory/data facts, authoritative Saudi sources control.
8. For current product features, record observation date.
9. For source code, pin exact revision before any adoption recommendation.
10. Maintain a rejection ledger so the same weak source is not repeatedly reconsidered.
