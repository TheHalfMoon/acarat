# Acarat Competitor and Capability Intelligence

**Status:** LIVE_RESEARCH_SNAPSHOT  
**Research date:** 2026-09-21  
**Purpose:** establish the current capability floor Acarat must exceed without copying proprietary product assets or assuming marketing claims are implementation evidence.

## 1. Strategic conclusion

Acarat cannot win by shipping:

- filters;
- map view;
- saved search alerts;
- AI chat;
- 360 tours;
- verified agents;
- WhatsApp contact;
- listing statistics.

These are already present across current Saudi/international platforms.

Acarat's differentiation should be the integrated decision and lifecycle system:

~~~text
DISCOVER
-> VERIFY
-> UNDERSTAND PRICE
-> UNDERSTAND AREA
-> COMPARE
-> SAVE/MONITOR
-> CONTACT
-> MANAGE CUSTOMER
-> VIEW
-> OFFER
-> CONTRACT
-> RENEW / MANAGE / RESALE
~~~

The defensible layer is the shared property/customer/agent/market evidence model across that lifecycle.

## 10. Acarat moat candidates

### 10.1 Saudi Property Passport

One source-aware view combining property, listing, address, agent, authority, price, market, POIs, commute, satellite, media, and history.

### 10.2 Acarat Fair Range

Rent/sale/land ranges with evidence, uncertainty, and comparable explorer.

### 10.3 Decision Map

God's-Eye-inspired layers adapted for real-estate decisions rather than decorative mapping.

### 10.4 Persistent customer intent

A search becomes a durable requirement that can power:

- alerts;
- comparison;
- Agent Terminal matching;
- viewing;
- future renewal/move.

### 10.5 Agent lifecycle OS

Past/current/future customer lifecycle, tasks, contract expiration, listing analytics, market intelligence, and government transaction status.

### 10.6 Transaction continuity

Acarat remains useful after contact and after contract through renewal/property management/resale.

## 11. Benchmark policy

Competitor marketing statements do not become Acarat parity claims.

For a parity or superiority claim:

1. define the exact capability;
2. capture current competitor public evidence;
3. define measurable Acarat behavior;
4. execute a matched benchmark/usability test;
5. record limitations;
6. avoid unsupported "best" claims.

The goal is to build the strongest product, not to make unverifiable superiority statements.
